# The tabs get a row of their own, flat as in VS Code (issue #488)

Reverses the tab placement of `2026-11-06-top-bar-search-beside-tabs.md` (issue #282), which put
the one area's tab strip in the top bar's row to save height at 720x480.

- **Why.** The user found the boxed tabs ugly. Measured at 1280x800, the top bar's row left the
  strip 298 px, and its tools took the rest down to 124 px for the tabs: two boxes reading "B." and
  "P.". No restyle fixes that room. VS Code and Orca both put the tabs on a row of their own.
- **What.** Each area holds its tab row (a split already did). Tabs are flat: 1 px separators,
  as wide as their title between 6 and 13 rem, inactive ones dimmer, the active one on the
  content's background with a 2 px accent line on top. The bar is a shade of `muted`.
- **Short windows.** Under 600 px tall (`SHORT_WINDOW` in `Areas.tsx`), the one area's strip goes
  back to the top bar's row. A 720x480 window keeps its conversation as before: the idle session's
  timeline measures 160 px in pt-BR, as on main (the layout journey's floor is 150).
- **The room for a pending request (#173's check).** The row costs 36 px, and #491's status bar
  costs 24 px more from 600 px up. While a request waits for the person (`session.pending`), the
  session header, the not-trusted notice and the summary take one line each (`TightArea` in
  `components/session/parts.tsx`; the short-area forms of #232 and #422). Measured on the demo's
  waiting session at 1280x800, with a 24 px bar simulated:

  | | timeline | pending card |
  |---|---|---|
  | main, no bar | 268–472 px (summary open or not) | 260 px |
  | this change, no bar | 378–489 px | 260 px |
  | this change, with the 24 px bar | 378 px, en and pt-BR | 260 px |

  CI's pt-BR header ran 44 px taller than the Pi's, which still leaves about 334 px.
